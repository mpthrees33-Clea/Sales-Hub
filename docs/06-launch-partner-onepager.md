## The moment you're invisible for today

Every week, an architect or designer's specified finish gets **discontinued, back-ordered past
the deadline, or value-engineered out**. In that scramble for an approved "or-equal," whoever the
designer happens to think of — or whoever their rep called back fastest — wins the spec. If
[Manufacturer] isn't top of mind in that 10-minute window, you lose a spec you could have won,
and you never even know it happened.

**That moment is the highest-intent point in the entire specification process. Nobody owns it.**

## What Or-Equal is

A tool that commercial designers use **free**: they drop in a product that won't work and instantly
get **verified, in-stock, in-budget, in-lead-time alternates** — matched on the things they
actually care about (color/LRV, pattern, finish, size, performance) and rendered right into their
project so they can see it before they spec it. Built by someone who's lived the commercial
finish-spec process from the rep side, for the A&D channel specifically — not a retail homeowner
widget.
