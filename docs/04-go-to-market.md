# 04 — Go-to-market (runway-aware)

**Constraint:** ~1–2 months of runway; founder building quietly to quit ASAP. Optimize for the
**fastest paid pilot**, using warm relationships — not cold marketing, not a polished platform.

## Chosen model: two-sided, Material-Bank-style

**Free for designers. Manufacturers pay.** Designers are the demand we *aggregate*, not the
side we bill. Manufacturers pay because getting specified is their revenue — the ROI is obvious
and the budget already exists (same reason Roomvo and Material Bank monetize the supply side).

The highest-value ad unit is the **discontinuation moment**: when a specified product dies and a
designer needs an or-equal *now*, that's the single highest-intent point in the entire spec
process. Being the default alternate there is something manufacturers will pay a lot for, and
nobody owns it today.

### The trap we must respect
Two-sided marketplaces are the **slowest, most capital-hungry** model. Manufacturers won't pay
until designers use it; designers won't use it until the catalog is deep. Material Bank
brute-forced that chicken-and-egg with massive VC + free overnight sample logistics. A *pure*
marketplace is the opposite of what 1–2 months of runway can survive. **Do not bet the next two
months on "build the marketplace and wait for factories to pay."**

### Our unfair edge — we skip the expensive part
The costliest part of a marketplace is acquiring the free side. **The founder *is* the
demand-side distribution** — a rep who sits with design firms every week. We can put the tool in
designers' hands for free, by hand, on the founder's own accounts, starting now: no ad spend, no
growth team. That de-risks the cold-start that normally requires VC.

## Protect the asset: trust (how "placement" must work)
The moment a designer suspects recommendations are **pay-to-play instead of best-match**, trust
collapses — and trust *is* the product (especially with verification). A recommendation engine is
more fragile than Material Bank's "a sample is a sample." So we sell manufacturers:

- **Inclusion + verified-partner status** — be in the index, be eligible, earn a trust badge.
- **Spec-intent data / leads** — "surfaced as the or-equal 40× this quarter"; "designers searched
  your category and you weren't in the index" (demand they're missing).
- **Clearly-labeled placement** — a "sponsored / also consider" slot, while the **merit ranking
  stays honest**.

We sell **access to the highest-intent moment + the data**, never the ability to displace a
better match.

## Sequencing (this is what saves the runway)

1. **Free tool → the founder's own designers first.** Cheap demand aggregation via relationships.
2. **Capture the data** — what's searched, what's discontinued, which or-equals win. This data is
   the product sold upstream.
3. **Sell manufacturers** on that intent + discontinuation placement — once real usage exists.
4. **Bridge cash in parallel** (below) so we don't starve before the flywheel spins.

## The bridge (near-term cash that also builds the marketplace)

- **Concierge service now:** charge a distributor/manufacturer to hand-run or-equal sourcing +
  renders on their hardest substitution requests. Proves manufacturer willingness to pay *today*
  and generates the exact data the free tool needs. (Service-before-software.)
- **One launch-partner manufacturer:** a contact who funds the build in exchange for being the
  featured partner in their categories while designer adoption grows. Bootstraps the paid side
  before a marketplace exists.

## What manufacturers actually buy (the ad units, recap)

| Unit | What it is | Why they pay |
|---|---|---|
| Verified-partner inclusion | In the index + trust badge | Eligibility to ever be surfaced |
| Discontinuation placement | Labeled "also consider" when a rival product dies | Highest-intent moment in spec |
| Spec-intent data / leads | Reports on searches, surfacings, wins, missed demand | Pipeline + product/road-map signal |
| Launch-partner / category sponsor | Funds build; featured in their categories | First-mover access while we grow |

## Metrics that make manufacturers pay (instrument from day one)

- # times a product is **surfaced as the or-equal**; # **specs won**.
- **Searched-but-not-in-index** demand (the "you're missing this" pitch).
- Designer engagement (renders generated, alternates accepted).

## Pricing posture (MBA hat)

- Concierge / launch-partner: **flat fee + short paid trial > free**. Even a small fee validates
  willingness to pay; no runway for charity.
- Anchor to value: one won spec or one saved blown-schedule substitution dwarfs a monthly fee.
- Land with one feature (whatever the spikes prove / makes a designer lean in), expand later.

## Open questions to resolve with the founder

- Which specific warm contact is the most likely **launch-partner manufacturer**?
- Which design firm(s) are the first free-designer beachhead (the founder's own accounts)?
- Non-compete / IP concerns with the current employer to navigate before selling?
- Where's the line on "labeled placement" the founder is comfortable with, trust-wise?
