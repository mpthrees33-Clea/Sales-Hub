# 04 — Go-to-market (runway-aware)

**Constraint:** ~1–2 months of runway; founder building quietly to quit ASAP. Optimize for the
**fastest paid pilot**, using warm relationships — not cold marketing, not a polished platform.

## The unfair advantage

The founder already talks to A&D firms, distributors, and manufacturers every week. First
customers come from that book of relationships. Don't build an audience — call the one you have.

## Who pays first (in priority order)

1. **A manufacturer or distributor pilot — the fastest real money.** They already fund
   visualization to get specified (it's why Roomvo gets paid). A manufacturer paying to have
   *their* catalog rendered into A&D presentations, or to be the preferred or-equal surfaced
   when a competitor's product dies, is a 4–5 figure deal. Founder knows who to call. **One
   pilot > 100 self-serve users**, and it fits the runway.
2. **Reps / showrooms / distributors — seats.** $99–299/mo per seat for a tool that wins specs.
   Founder is the reference user.
3. **A&D firms — later.** Higher trust bar (verification must be airtight); pursue after a
   manufacturer pilot proves the engine.

## Sequencing

- **Now:** run both spikes (de-risk). In parallel, line up **one warm pilot conversation**.
- **Wks 2–3:** scrappy MVP good enough for the founder to use **live on real accounts**. Manual
  back-end is fine. Capture reactions = validation + testimonials.
- **Wks 4–6:** close the first paid pilot. Let what made people lean in decide what to productize.

## Pricing posture (MBA hat)

- **Pilot:** a flat setup fee + a short paid trial > free pilots (free pilots don't validate
  willingness to pay; you have no runway for charity). Even a small fee proves the wedge.
- **Anchor to value:** a single won spec or a saved blown-schedule substitution dwarfs a monthly
  seat fee. Price against the deal, not against software comps.
- **Land with one feature** (whichever spike wins / whichever made a designer's eyes light up),
  expand into the full loop later.

## Fastest-cash fallback (if the platform needs more runway than we have)

Sell the **service** before the software: manually run the or-equal search + render for a
distributor's hardest substitution requests, by hand, for a fee. Proves demand, generates cash,
and produces the exact data/labels the platform needs. (This is the bridge that protects the
runway while the product matures.)

## Open questions to resolve with the founder

- Which specific warm contact is the most likely first pilot?
- Manufacturer-funded (they pay to be surfaced/rendered) vs. seat-based (reps/designers pay)?
- Is there a non-compete / IP concern with the current employer to navigate before selling?
