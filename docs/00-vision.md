# 00 — Vision

## The problem (from inside the trade)

In commercial interiors, an architect or designer specifies finishes — tile, stone, LVT,
porcelain, architectural finish films — by exact product. Then reality hits:

- The product gets **discontinued**, or a *size/finish within a series* gets dropped.
- The **lead time** blows the schedule (12–16 weeks when the job needs it in 4).
- The price moved, or the project value-engineers and needs a cheaper look-alike.

When that happens, someone scrambles for an **"or-equal"** — an approved substitute that
matches the design intent and the performance spec. Today that scramble is manual: calling
reps, digging through binders and PDFs, guessing at what's in stock. It's slow, it's where
deals and schedules die, and it's a job the people doing it would happily pay to make disappear.

## Why this is a real wedge (not another Roomvo)

The existing visualization tools — **Roomvo** (Leap Tools), TilesView, TilesDisplay, Wizart,
Viz2D — are built for the **retail consumer**: a homeowner on a flooring retailer's website
swapping a floor texture in a room photo. Mature, funded, crowded.

**Nobody owns the commercial A&D spec channel.** That's the gap:

- Walls + complex layouts (large-format, herringbone, mosaics) + **architectural finish films**.
- Rendering onto **drawings/elevations**, not just phone photos.
- The **rep/designer as the user**, fast enough to run live in a CEU / lunch-and-learn or a
  design meeting to *win the spec*.
- A real **alternate-finder** tied to availability and lead time — which no retail widget does.

## Why now

  in-context visualization possible.
- Cheap image **embeddings + color science** make explainable visual matching practical.
- Agentic crawling makes a self-updating catalog maintainable by a small team.

> available, in-budget alternative they can *see* in their own project — and spec with confidence.
